CRM (Customer Relationship Management)

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. It encompasses the strategies, technologies, and tools that companies use to manage and analyze customer interactions and data throughout the customer lifecycle.

Key Features of Modern CRM Systems

  • Contact Management
  • Sales Pipeline Management
  • Marketing Automation
  • Customer Service Tools
  • Analytics and Reporting
